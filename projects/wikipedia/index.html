Duck Donuts is an American doughnut shop chain based near Mechanicsburg, Pennsylvania. It started in Duck, North Carolina, in 2006 and has grown to more than 120 shops. The chain is known for customizable, made-to-order doughnuts.

History

Duck Donuts was founded by Russ DiGilio and Robin Griffith, two Delaware County, Pennsylvania natives who met in 1992 while working in the assisted living industry. Before the brand existed, the Outer Banks of North Carolina had no doughnut shops.

The first shop opened in Duck, North Carolina, which gave the chain its name. Shops in Kitty Hawk, Kill Devil Hills and Corolla followed.

Growing Beyond the Outer Banks

For about five years the company stayed in the Outer Banks. After many people asked about franchising, the company created a franchising arm, and the first franchise opened in Williamsburg, Virginia, in 2013. Shops later appeared in Florida, New Jersey, Georgia, New York, California and Minnesota. International locations include Puerto Rico, Thailand and the Philippines.

The Menu

The menu is mostly doughnuts, breakfast sandwiches, coffee and desserts. The cake doughnuts are made to order, and the doughnut-making process is automated with machines from Belshaw Adamatic.
